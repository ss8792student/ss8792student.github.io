1 Books

A first library for my dad's old school

I started Books Without Borders, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, to get books to schools that don't have enough of them. Our first project was Readathon Revolution, a readathon and community book drive.

It paid for the first library at a government school in Telangana, India, and for the school's newspaper subscriptions.

2 Letters

An app for the letters that are hardest to read

Official letters were the hardest part of English for my parents, long after everyday conversation got easy. So I built Fine Print with a teammate for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.

You photograph a letter and it explains it in plain language, in more than 30 languages. A second AI model checks the first, because AI can sound confident and still be wrong.

3 Judges

Research on the models that do the grading

That checker came out of my research. LLM judges are models that grade other models' answers, and a lot of AI evaluation now depends on them.

On my Algoverse team, we test whether a judge's verdict holds up under pressure. On my own, I'm testing whether judges mark down answers that mix Telugu and English.
